Canon Error P07 and Support Code 5B00

Some Canon PIXMA and G-Series printers can display Error P07 together with support codes such as 5B00 or 5B01.

These errors are associated with printer service conditions and can be related to the waste ink or ink absorber system on supported models.

Canon also uses separate warning codes when an ink absorber is approaching capacity, so the exact message and procedure should always be checked for the specific printer model.

Canon Ink Absorber and Waste Ink System

Inkjet printers perform print-head cleaning and maintenance operations. Waste ink generated during these processes must be collected inside the printer.

The absorber system contains material designed to hold this waste ink. Over time, the printer may report that the absorber requires service attention.

Resetting a counter does not physically remove accumulated ink. The absorber and surrounding waste ink system should be inspected and serviced appropriately.

Important: Canon Models Are Different

The UniTech LK video on this page demonstrates the Canon PIXMA G3416.

Do not assume that the absorber location, disassembly procedure, service mode or waste ink counter reset is identical on every Canon printer.

Always check instructions for the exact Canon model before opening the printer or performing service-mode operations.

Canon Printer Frequently Asked Questions

What does Canon Error P07 mean?

On supported Canon models, P07 can appear together with support codes such as 5B00 or 5B01 when the printer reports a service condition.

What does Canon Support Code 5B00 mean?

5B00 is associated with a printer service condition involving the waste ink or ink absorber system on supported Canon models.

What is the Canon ink absorber?

It is part of the printer's waste ink collection system and absorbs ink produced during print-head cleaning and other maintenance operations.

Can I remove the Canon ink absorber myself?

Access and removal vary by model. The UniTech LK video demonstrates the Canon G3416 only. Other Canon printers may require a different disassembly and servicing procedure.

Does resetting 5B00 clean the absorber?

No. Resetting an electronic waste ink counter does not physically remove waste ink from an absorber.

Can the Canon 5B00 error return?

Yes. If the physical waste ink condition has not been addressed, or the printer requires further service, the problem can return.

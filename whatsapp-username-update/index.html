WhatsApp Username Update — How WhatsApp Usernames Work

Learn about WhatsApp usernames, what the new username feature means, how availability can vary during rollout and how usernames can provide another way to identify accounts.

Published: July 4, 2026

What Is the WhatsApp Username Feature?

WhatsApp has been developing username functionality as another way for users to identify and connect with accounts.

This is particularly important because WhatsApp has traditionally relied heavily on phone numbers when people connect with one another.

WhatsApp Username Explained

A username provides an account identifier that is different from simply displaying a profile name.

This can make it easier to identify or connect with an account without relying only on the visible phone number where the feature is supported.

Why the WhatsApp Username Option May Not Appear

WhatsApp frequently introduces major features gradually.

This means two people using WhatsApp at the same time may not always see exactly the same features.

Availability can depend on factors such as:

  • WhatsApp version
  • Account rollout
  • Device platform
  • Testing stage
  • Regional availability

Keep WhatsApp Updated

If you are waiting for a new WhatsApp feature, keep the official WhatsApp application updated through your device's supported application store.

Updating the app does not necessarily mean every server-side feature will become available immediately.

WhatsApp Username vs Phone Number

A username and a phone number should not be treated as exactly the same thing.

WhatsApp accounts continue to use phone numbers for registration, while username functionality can provide an additional account identifier where available.

Protect Your WhatsApp Account

Do not share verification codes, registration codes or two-step verification PINs with anyone claiming that they can reserve or activate a WhatsApp username for you.

Use only official WhatsApp applications and supported account settings.

WhatsApp Username Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't I have a WhatsApp username yet?

Feature availability can vary during a staged rollout. Having the latest WhatsApp version does not necessarily guarantee immediate access to every new feature.

Is a WhatsApp username the same as my profile name?

No. A profile display name and a unique account username serve different purposes.

Do I still need a phone number?

WhatsApp accounts continue to use phone numbers for registration. Username functionality is an additional account feature rather than a replacement for the registration process.

Should I pay someone to reserve my WhatsApp username?

Be cautious of anyone requesting money, verification codes or account credentials in exchange for activating or reserving a username.

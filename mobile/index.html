WhatsApp Troubleshooting

WhatsApp problems can involve app updates, operating-system compatibility, device support, account features or staged feature rollouts.

Always check the official application version and the operating-system version installed on your phone.

iPhone Troubleshooting

Older iPhones can eventually stop receiving major operating-system updates, which can affect support for newer application versions.

Check the installed iOS version and device model before assuming the application itself is broken.

Mobile Account Safety

Do not share verification codes, passwords, two-step verification PINs or account recovery information with anyone offering to activate a feature for you.

Use official application stores and official account settings.
